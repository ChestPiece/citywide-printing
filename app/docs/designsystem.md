# City Wide Printing Services

## Final Brand Identity & Design System

**Location:** M-37, Mussafah, Abu Dhabi
**Business:** Printing, photocopying, typing and large-format printing
**Primary objective:** Turn local search traffic and referrals into WhatsApp enquiries, calls and store visits.

---

# 01. Brand Direction

## Core Concept

### Precision Local

City Wide combines:

* **Precision Industrial** visual language
* **Contemporary Utility** conversion philosophy

The brand should feel:

> **Precise. Practical. Modern. Local.**

It should look like a business that takes the quality of its work seriously, while remaining approachable to someone who simply needs something printed today.

### Brand Personality

| Trait        | Expression                                        |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Precise      | Clean grids, alignment, structured layouts        |
| Reliable     | Consistent typography, restrained visual language |
| Practical    | Short copy, obvious CTAs, useful information      |
| Modern       | Contemporary typography and spacious layouts      |
| Local        | Strong Mussafah positioning and real photography  |
| Professional | No gimmicks, exaggerated claims or visual clutter |

### What the brand is NOT

Avoid making City Wide look:

* Like a SaaS startup
* Like a creative agency
* Like a luxury fashion brand
* Like a cheap neighborhood copy shop
* Like a giant commercial printing corporation
* Overly corporate
* Overly colorful
* Dependent on generic stock photography

The sweet spot is:

**Premium presentation + everyday practicality.**

---

# 02. Positioning

## Primary Positioning

**Professional printing and document services in M-37, Mussafah.**

The website should immediately answer:

1. What do they do?
2. Where are they?
3. How can I contact them?

### Positioning Statement

> City Wide Printing Services provides printing, photocopying, typing and large-format printing from M-37, Mussafah, Abu Dhabi.

This is intentionally factual.

Do not manufacture claims such as:

* “Abu Dhabi's #1 printer”
* “The fastest printing service”
* “The cheapest prices”
* “Trusted by thousands”
* “Industry-leading quality”

unless independently verified.

---

# 03. Visual Philosophy

## Design Principle

### "Printed matter, engineered for clarity."

The visual system takes subtle inspiration from the physical printing process:

* Paper
* Grids
* Crop marks
* Registration marks
* Measurement systems
* Print sheets
* Ink
* Layers
* Folding
* Alignment
* Large-format output

These references should remain **subtle**.

The website should not look like a Photoshop mockup or a design-school portfolio.

### Visual Rule

**80% clean interface
20% print-inspired detail**

That ratio keeps the brand premium rather than gimmicky.

The brand should feel engineered rather than soft.

### Border

`1px solid #E6E4DE`

# 08. Graphic Language

## Core Graphic Elements

Use these sparingly throughout the site.

### Registration Marks

Small crosshair-style marks inspired by print registration.

Use around:

* Hero imagery
* Section transitions
* Large typography
* Featured services

---

### Crop Marks

Small L-shaped marks.

Use as decorative framing around selected content.

---

### Measurement Lines

Subtle lines with labels such as:

```text
1200 MM
A4
A3
M-37
```

These should function as visual texture rather than fake technical specifications.

---

### Grid Lines

Very subtle grid overlays can appear behind:

* Hero
* Large images
* Dark sections

Opacity should remain extremely low.

---

### Paper Layers

Use overlapping rectangles to suggest:

* Printed sheets
* Flyers
* Documents
* Paper stacks

Keep the treatment geometric and restrained.

# 11. Logo Treatment

The existing City Wide identity should remain the source of truth if an official logo is available.

Do not redesign the logo merely for the sake of the landing page.

### Recommended treatment

On light backgrounds:

**Dark logo**

On dark backgrounds:

**Light/reversed logo**

Accent blue may be used selectively if it already aligns with the official identity.

### Clear Space

Maintain generous breathing room around the logo.

Do not:

* Add unnecessary shadows
* Stretch the logo
* Rotate it
* Place it over busy photography
* Add effects
* Put it inside decorative containers without purpose

**WhatsApp Your Requirement**

This should be the dominant conversion action.

They may simply have:

# 21. Motion System

Motion should communicate precision.

### Use

* Subtle fade-ins
* Small upward reveals
* Image masking
* Smooth hover states
* Slight card translation
* Button feedback

### Avoid

* Excessive parallax
* Large spinning animations
* Constant movement
* Particle backgrounds
* Bouncy animations
* Overly cinematic transitions

Animation duration:

**180–400ms**

The interface should feel controlled.

# 26. Brand Voice

## Tone

**Direct. Clear. Practical. Confident.**

The brand should sound like a local business that knows its work, not a marketing department.

### Good

> Need something printed?

> Send us your requirement on WhatsApp.

> Printing, photocopying, typing and large-format printing in M-37, Mussafah.

### Bad

> We deliver unparalleled printing solutions that empower businesses to achieve their communication goals.

That says nothing.

---

# 27. Copy Rules

### Rule 1 — Be specific

Say:

> Printing and photocopying

instead of:

> Comprehensive document solutions

### Rule 2 — Remove corporate filler

Avoid:

* Solutions
* Excellence
* Empower
* Seamless
* Cutting-edge
* World-class
* Innovative
* End-to-end
* Bespoke

unless the wording is genuinely necessary.

### Rule 3 — Don't exaggerate

No unsupported claims.

### Rule 4 — Keep paragraphs short

Most sections should communicate their point in **1–3 sentences**.

### Rule 5 — Every major section needs a purpose

If a section does not help the customer understand, trust, or contact the business, remove it.

Premium does **not** mean adding more effects.

For City Wide, premium comes from:

* Strong typography
* Consistent spacing

* Restrained color
